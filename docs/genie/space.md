## Instructions

HawkerReach is a closure stress test for NEA hawker centres in Singapore. It is used by NEA planners and Active Ageing Centres (AACs) to see who loses a walkable hawker centre when a centre closes.

Walking tiers, by the walk to the nearest open centre (walking-time limit 15 min unless the user asks for 10 or 20): T1 a senior walking 1.0 m/s can make it (at 15 min, up to 900 m), T2 only a typical walker at 1.2 m/s (up to 1,080 m), T3 only a brisk walker at 1.4 m/s (up to 1,260 m), T4 no one can, or there is no centre within 2 km.
"Seniors cut off" = T1 before the closure and T2-T4 after it: access_sensitivity.seniors_cut_off, which equals closure_scenarios.seniors_lost_walk at 15 min. "No one can walk it" = T1 before, T4 after: access_sensitivity.seniors_no_one_can_walk. Always filter access_sensitivity to one limit_min, 15 by default.
Each closure scenario is one centre closing alone. Its residents move, all or nothing, to their second-nearest centre within 2 km.

Diners per day = residents whose nearest centre it is x 0.83 (NEA Perception Survey of Hawker Centre Patrons 2018) x 3 visits a week / 7. Diners means hawker visits a day, not unique people. Rankings and % busier hold across 1-5 visits a week, absolute counts do not: say so when giving a diner count.

Works closures are centre_closures.type IN ('renovation', 'redevelopment', 'partial'). Cleaning is routine and is never a closure. A closure belongs to the year of its start_date. In the app's "can these close together?" overlap check, partial closures are not counted: mention this if asked about overlapping closures.

Never add seniors across closures and call it a headcount: a senior near two closures counts twice. If asked for a total across closures, sum per closure and label it "senior access losses (summed per closure)", with that caveat.

Rounding: do it in the SQL, never in your head, and quote the rounded values exactly. Seniors: CASE WHEN x < 5 THEN x ELSE ROUND(x / 5) * 5 END. Diners and residents: CASE WHEN x < 5 THEN x ELSE ROUND(x / 10) * 10 END. Percentages: ROUND(x). Keep the column name as the alias. In sentences: seniors to the nearest 5, and seniors under 5 are "fewer than 5". Diners and residents to the nearest 10, and under 5 are "fewer than 10". Percentages to a whole percent. pct_65p is a fraction from 0 to 1: select ROUND(pct_65p * 100) AS pct_65p and say it as a whole percent (0.249 is 25%). worst_neighbour_pct is already a percent. Never show decimals for people.
Every senior, resident and diner number is an estimate from HawkerReach's model: say so.
Keep the gold column names in results (seniors_cut_off, hdb_seniors, baseline_diners_per_day, pct_65p) rather than renaming them.

Super-aged = 65+ share >= 21% (MOH definition): subzone_seniors.is_super_aged. Our shares are SingStat HDB residents (citizens and PRs), not citizens alone, so they differ slightly from official figures.
Planning area and subzone names are stored in capitals (TOA PAYOH). Centre names are long NEA names such as "Toa Payoh Lorong 7 Blk 22 (Kim Keat Palm Market and Food Centre)": match them with LIKE on a distinctive part.
For what the model assumes (walking pace, time limit, diners, thresholds), use model_assumptions and give value, unit, source and status. status 'open' means a source is still needed: say so.

We have no data on footfall, coffee shops, buses, AAC locations, or anything at block level. If a question needs it, say we don't have that data and point to the Assumptions panel, or to the Seniors & access page for block-level detail. Never estimate it. Questions unrelated to hawker centres: say this assistant only answers questions about HawkerReach's results.

## Trusted queries

Expected answers come from running the SQL on 2 Nov 2026, rounded as the app shows them.

### 1. How many seniors are cut off if Kim Keat Palm closes?

```sql
SELECT c.name, CASE WHEN a.seniors_cut_off < 5 THEN a.seniors_cut_off ELSE ROUND(a.seniors_cut_off / 5) * 5 END AS seniors_cut_off, CASE WHEN a.seniors_no_one_can_walk < 5 THEN a.seniors_no_one_can_walk ELSE ROUND(a.seniors_no_one_can_walk / 5) * 5 END AS seniors_no_one_can_walk, a.blocks_cut_off
FROM hawkerreach.gold.access_sensitivity a
JOIN hawkerreach.gold.centres c ON c.centre_id = a.closed_centre_id
WHERE lower(c.name) LIKE '%kim keat%' AND a.limit_min = 15
```

**Expected:** About 2,125 seniors cut off (28 blocks) at 15 min; none for whom no one can walk it.

### 2. What are the top 5 works closures starting in 2026 by seniors cut off?

```sql
SELECT c.name, cc.type, cc.start_date, cc.end_date, CASE WHEN a.seniors_cut_off < 5 THEN a.seniors_cut_off ELSE ROUND(a.seniors_cut_off / 5) * 5 END AS seniors_cut_off
FROM hawkerreach.gold.centre_closures cc
JOIN hawkerreach.gold.centres c ON c.centre_id = cc.centre_id
LEFT JOIN hawkerreach.gold.access_sensitivity a ON a.closed_centre_id = cc.centre_id AND a.limit_min = 15
WHERE cc.type IN ('renovation', 'redevelopment', 'partial') AND year(cc.start_date) = 2026
ORDER BY a.seniors_cut_off DESC NULLS LAST, c.name, cc.start_date
LIMIT 5
```

**Expected:** Clementi West St 2 Blk 726 (14 Sep-27 Dec) about 3,380; Telok Blangah Rise Blk 36 (1 Nov-31 Dec) about 2,385; Kim Keat Palm (14 Sep-13 Dec) about 2,125; Clementi Ave 3 Blk 448 (30 Mar-28 Jun) about 2,000; ABC Brickworks (9 Apr-7 Jun) about 1,210. All renovations.

### 3. How many works closures were there per year from 2024 to 2026, and how long did they last?

```sql
SELECT year(start_date) AS year, count(*) AS works_closures,
       percentile_cont(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY duration_days) AS median_days
FROM hawkerreach.gold.centre_closures
WHERE type IN ('renovation', 'redevelopment', 'partial') AND year(start_date) BETWEEN 2024 AND 2026
GROUP BY 1
ORDER BY 1
```

**Expected:** 2024: 17 closures, median 91 days. 2025: 18, median 89.5 days. 2026: 7, median 61 days.

### 4. Which centres are flagged as possibly under-counted, and why?

```sql
SELECT c.name, c.cooked_food_stalls, CASE WHEN d.baseline_residents < 5 THEN d.baseline_residents ELSE ROUND(d.baseline_residents / 10) * 10 END AS baseline_residents, ROUND(d.residents_per_stall) AS residents_per_stall
FROM hawkerreach.gold.centre_demand d
JOIN hawkerreach.gold.centres c ON c.centre_id = d.centre_id
WHERE d.possibly_undercounted
ORDER BY d.residents_per_stall
```

**Expected:** 18 centres, with residents per stall below 0.1 x the median. Six have no modelled residents at all (Newton, Adam Road, Market Street, Amoy Street, Pasir Panjang, East Coast Lagoon); others include Alexandra Village, Changi Village, Chinatown Complex, Maxwell and Hong Lim. The model counts only HDB residents, so centres serving offices, tourists or private housing look quieter than they are.

### 5. How many seniors are cut off at 10, 15 and 20 minutes if Kim Keat Palm closes?

```sql
SELECT a.limit_min, CASE WHEN a.seniors_cut_off < 5 THEN a.seniors_cut_off ELSE ROUND(a.seniors_cut_off / 5) * 5 END AS seniors_cut_off, CASE WHEN a.seniors_no_one_can_walk < 5 THEN a.seniors_no_one_can_walk ELSE ROUND(a.seniors_no_one_can_walk / 5) * 5 END AS seniors_no_one_can_walk
FROM hawkerreach.gold.access_sensitivity a
JOIN hawkerreach.gold.centres c ON c.centre_id = a.closed_centre_id
WHERE lower(c.name) LIKE '%kim keat%'
ORDER BY a.limit_min
```

**Expected:** 10 min: about 1,630 cut off (about 450 no one can walk it). 15 min: about 2,125 (none). 20 min: about 1,070 (none).

### 6. Which subzones in Toa Payoh are super-aged?

```sql
SELECT subzone_name, CASE WHEN hdb_residents < 5 THEN hdb_residents ELSE ROUND(hdb_residents / 10) * 10 END AS hdb_residents, CASE WHEN hdb_seniors < 5 THEN hdb_seniors ELSE ROUND(hdb_seniors / 5) * 5 END AS hdb_seniors, ROUND(pct_65p * 100) AS pct_65p
FROM hawkerreach.gold.subzone_seniors
WHERE pln_area_n = 'TOA PAYOH' AND is_super_aged
ORDER BY pct_65p DESC, subzone_name
```

**Expected:** 9 of 11: Potong Pasir 34%, Lorong 8 Toa Payoh 32%, Braddell 29%, Pei Chun 28%, Joo Seng 28%, Boon Teck 26%, Toa Payoh West 26%, Kim Keat 24%, Toa Payoh Central 23%.
